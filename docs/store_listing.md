## Full description (≤4000 chars)

Take compliant passport, visa and ID photos at home — completely free, completely offline.

PhotoPass turns any photo into a correctly sized, print-ready passport photo in a few taps. Pick your country and document type, take a photo with the on-screen face guide (or choose one from your gallery), adjust the crop, and export a compliant image, a print sheet or a PDF.

WHY PHOTO PASS?

• TRULY FREE — no ads, no subscriptions, no "Pro" version, no watermarks. Every feature is free forever.
• PRIVATE BY DESIGN — your photos never leave your phone. There is no account, no cloud upload, no analytics and no tracking. The app works fully offline.
• OFFICIAL SPECS — sizes and rules come from official sources (e.g. 35×45 mm for US passports, Schengen visa rules), each with its source link and verification date inside the app.
• SMART FACE GUIDE — the camera overlay helps you position your head correctly before you shoot.
• AUTO CROP — face detection suggests the perfect crop so the head and eye line fall inside the official range.
• BACKGROUND CLEAN-UP — replace a cluttered background with the plain white or blue the authorities require, and fine-tune brightness, contrast and colour.
• COMPLIANCE CHECK — a clear checklist compares your photo with the official requirements: size, one face, head height, eye line, background colour, file size.
• EXPORT ANYTHING — exact-size JPEG for online forms, multi-photo print sheets (10×15 cm, A4) for the drugstore, and a print-ready PDF at the exact physical size.
• 15 LANGUAGES — English, Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, Farsi, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Turkish, Urdu. Full right-to-left support.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Choose your country and document type.
2. Take a photo with the face guide, or pick one from your gallery.
3. Crop, clean up the background and check compliance.
4. Save to your gallery, share, or export a print sheet / PDF.

OFFLINE & OPEN

All processing — face detection, cropping, background replacement, compliance checks, print layouts, PDFs — happens on your device. The only internet access is tapping an official source link, which opens in your browser. Requirements change over time; every spec shows when it was last verified and links to the official source so you can double-check.

PhotoPass cannot guarantee that an authority will accept any photo, but it gives you the tools to meet the published requirements and shows exactly what was measured.
