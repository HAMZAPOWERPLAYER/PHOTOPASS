# Privacy Policy — PhotoPass

**Last updated: 15 October 2026**

PhotoPass is a photo utility app for passport, visa and ID photos. This
privacy policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your data:
nothing.

## Summary

Your photos never leave your device. PhotoPass has no servers, no accounts
and no analytics. The app works fully offline.

## What data do we collect?

None. PhotoPass does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any
personal data. There is no sign-up, no login and no advertising identifier.

## What happens to my photos?

Photos you take or import are processed entirely on your device (face
detection, cropping, background replacement, compliance checks, print sheets
and PDF generation all run locally). They are stored only in the app's
private storage on your phone and are only ever shared when **you** tap
"Save photo", "Share" or an export button — at that point the file is handed
to your phone's own gallery or share sheet.

Deleting the app removes all of its data. You can also delete individual
photos or all saved photos from inside the app (Settings → Delete all saved
photos).

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to take your photo. Frames are processed on-device.
- **Photos (system picker)** — to let you choose an existing photo. The app
  uses the system photo picker and cannot browse your whole library.

## Network activity

PhotoPass itself makes no network requests. The only way any internet
connection is used is when you tap an "Official source" link for a country
specification, which opens in your web browser. Government websites you
visit there are subject to their own privacy policies.

## Ads, tracking and third parties

PhotoPass contains no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no
tracking and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. The complete list of
open-source packages the app uses is in the app's source repository
(`pubspec.yaml`), and an automated test in the repository fails the build if
a tracking, advertising or billing package is ever added.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly process any personal
data of anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the app and
be available in the repository. Given the app's architecture, any change
that would introduce data collection would be a fundamental change and would
be called out prominently.
